# Deck (client or stakeholder)

Outline first, 5–7 slides, then build it with the slides/pptx skill.

1. **Title:** the question the deck answers, the date, the audience.
2. **The answer:** one sentence + 3 supporting numbers.
3. **What it is** (build: the parts and what each does for the audience) or **what changed** (improvement: at most 4 changes, each with its outcome). Never the steps of the work.
4. **Results:** one chart or table against the best previous baseline.
5. **Cost:** per unit and at the client's scale (e.g. per 100 000 requests), with the price source and its date.
6. **Risks and open questions:** each with a proposed way forward.
7. **Next steps:** owners and dates.

One message per slide, the headline states the takeaway ("Cost per request halved", not
"Cost"). Speaker notes carry the detail. No customer data on slides.
